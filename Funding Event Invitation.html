DefenceTech Funding · Kyiv 2026

Civitta, in partnership with Ukrainian Council of Defense Industry, brings you

Ukrainian DefenceTech: it’s time to access the funding
that powers your mission

Join us for a half-day session dedicated to exploring grant funding opportunities for defense tech companies. Topics will include the European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP) – a €1.5 billion initiative offering grants of up to €30M to scale production, with a dedicated Ukraine Support Instrument for Ukrainian companies – as well as European Defence Fund (EDF) grants and other funding mechanisms.
